# Code of Conduct (`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`)

## Our Pledge

We are committed to fostering an open, rigorous, welcoming, and harassment-free scientific and engineering community around **CELLULOGONIC MECHA**, regardless of background, institutional affiliation (or independent status), experience level, or discipline.

## Expected Standards

- **Scientific Rigor & Honesty:** Critique proofs, code, statistical methodology, and claims incisively and evidence-first without personal attacks.
- **Respectful Discourse:** Welcome replication attempts (especially negative results and counterexamples), credit prior work accurately, and communicate constructively.
- **Unacceptable Behavior:** Harassment, doxxing, deliberate fabrication of data or citations, or abusive conduct will not be tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `0_0@0thernes.art` (Alexander Stephen Donahue). All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant v2.1.
