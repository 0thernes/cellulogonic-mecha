# CELLULOGONIC MECHA v1.0 — Comprehensive Glossary

Alphabetical reference of **62 foundational terms** spanning cellular biology, biochemistry, membrane biophysics, microbiology, eutelic metazoan anatomy, cryptobiosis, xenobiology, non-equilibrium thermodynamics, formal verification, and Artificial Life.

---

### A
- **Abiogenesis (Protocell Condensation)**: The transition from abiotic non-living prebiotic chemistry to self-bounded, metabolizing, dividing cellular vesicles without pre-seeded living progenitors.
- **Active Operon Length ($L_{\text{eff}}$)**: The number of functional genomic loci actively driving non-zero catalytic, transport, morphogenetic, or survival pathways above knockout threshold.
- **Amphiphile**: A molecule possessing both a hydrophilic headgroup and hydrophobic tail(s) (e.g., fatty acids, phospholipids, archaeal tetraethers, or cryogenic acrylonitrile azotosomes) that self-assembles into closed bilayers or monolayers above the Critical Micelle Concentration (CMC).
- **Anhydrobiosis**: Reversible ametabolic dormancy triggered by extreme desiccation (>95–98% water loss), famously mastered by tardigrades via intrinsically disordered bioglass proteins (TDP/CAHS).
- **Antiporter (ATP/ADP Translocase)**: A transmembrane carrier protein exchanging intracellular organelle ATP for host cytoplasmic ADP+Pᵢ — the essential biochemical handshake stabilizing endosymbiosis against selfish organelle parasitism.
- **Apoptosis (Constitutional Policing)**: Programmed cellular self-destruction triggered by damage or multicellular policing signals to eliminate selfish somatic cancer defectors.
- **Archaea**: One of the two prokaryotic domains of life, distinguished by ether-linked isoprenoid membrane lipids (often membrane-spanning GDGT tetraether monolayers), unique RNA polymerases, and extreme thermophilic/acidophilic/halophilic tolerance.
- **Assembly Index ($A$)**: From Assembly Theory (Sharma, Cronin & Walker, 2023), the minimum number of joining steps required to construct a molecular or compositional object from elementary building blocks; high-copy ($N \ge 4$) elevated $A$ separates evolutionary selection from random noise.
- **ATP Synthase ($\text{F}_1\text{F}_0$ Rotary Motor)**: Nanoscale rotary turbine embedded in energy-transducing membranes that converts transmembrane proton-motive force ($\Delta p$) into phosphoanhydride ATP bonds using a c-ring of $n_H$ proton-binding subunits.
- **Autopoiesis**: Maturana & Varela's (1974) formal characterization of a living system as a bounded network of reactions that continuously regenerates both its internal components and its own enclosing boundary.

### B
- **Bedau–Packard Evolutionary Activity ($A_{\text{new}}$)**: Quantitative open-ended evolution metric measuring the rate at which novel adaptive lineages persist and propagate beyond a neutral-shadow baseline.
- **Bioelectric Morphogenesis**: Michael Levin's experimentally grounded principle that spatial patterns of transmembrane resting voltage ($V_m$) coupled via gap junctions store and broadcast anatomical target morphology across multicellular tissues.
- **Bioglass (Amorphous Vitrification)**: Non-crystalline solid glass matrix formed by tardigrade-specific intrinsically disordered proteins (TDPs / CAHS) upon dehydration, locking membranes and enzymes in place without damaging ice or salt crystals.

### C
- **CAHS / SAHS / MAHS (Tardigrade Disordered Proteins)**: Cytosolic-, Secreted-, and Mitochondrial-Abundant Heat Soluble intrinsically disordered proteins responsible for tardigrade desiccation tolerance (Boothby et al., 2017).
- **Canham–Helfrich Bending Energy**: The continuum elastic free energy of a closed lipid bilayer vesicle, $E_{\text{bend}} = \frac{\kappa}{2}\int (2H - c_0)^2 dA + \bar{\kappa}\int K\,dA$, governing red-blood-cell shapes, budding, and spontaneous protocell fission.
- **Cellulogonic Mecha**: Build 2 (CELLULAR) of the 0thernes 4-build ALife ecosystem — a clean-room integer-quantum Chemoton, elastic membrane, chemiosmotic, endosymbiotic, bioelectric, and xenocellular simulator verified by 44 Lean 4 proofs and 20 falsification gates.
- **Chemiosmosis**: Peter Mitchell's (1961) Nobel-winning mechanism coupling transmembrane electron transport to proton/ion pumping ($\Delta p = \Delta \Psi - \frac{2.3RT}{F}\Delta\text{pH}$), which in turn drives rotary ATP synthesis.
- **Chemoton**: Tibor Gánti's (1975, 2003) minimal stoichiometric fluid automaton unifying three coupled autocatalytic subsystems: a metabolic cycle, a genetic template polymer, and an enclosing self-growing membrane.
- **Chloroplast (Plastid / Proto-Chloroplast)**: Photosynthetic double-membrane organelle descended from engulfed cyanobacteria (primary endosymbiosis) or engulfed eukaryotic algae (secondary/tertiary endosymbiosis).
- **COVI (Cellular Omega Vitality Index)**: Weighted 8-pillar (`0..100`) cellular vitality metric gated by anti-gaming (inert crystal / white-noise zero-bounds, Lean `T34`–`T35`) and Spiegelman complexity-collapse penalties.
- **Critical Micelle Concentration (CMC)**: The threshold amphiphile concentration above which free monomers spontaneously self-assemble into micelles and closed bilayer vesicles.
- **c-Ring Stoichiometry ($n_H$)**: The number of protons required per synthesized ATP molecule; thermodynamically bounded by $n_H \cdot e_{\text{pmf}} \ge \Delta G_{\text{ATP}}$ (Lean `T28`–`T30`).
- **Cryptobiosis**: Reversible suspension of measurable metabolic activity under extreme environmental stress (anhydrobiosis, cryobiosis, osmobiosis, anoxybiosis).
- **Cytoskeleton**: Intracellular polymeric force-generating scaffold — actin/myosin, microtubules/kinesin/dynein, intermediate filaments in eukaryotes; FtsZ (division ring) and MreB in prokaryotes.

### D
- **Dobzhansky–Muller Barrier**: Post-zygotic reproductive incompatibility arising when independently evolved alleles or genetic/ring architectures fail to recombine viably.
- **Dsup (Damage Suppressor Protein)**: Tardigrade-specific chromatin-binding protein that physically shields nuclear DNA from hydroxyl-radical and X-ray radiation damage (Hashimoto et al., 2016).

### E
- **Endosymbiosis (Serial Endosymbiosis Theory)**: Lynn Margulis's (1967) mechanism by which free-living prokaryotes are engulfed and retained inside a host cell as permanent nested organelles (mitochondria, plastids), driving eukaryogenesis.
- **Endosymbiotic Gene Transfer (EGT)**: Evolutionary migration of genes from an internalized organelle genome (mtDNA/cpDNA) into the host nuclear genome, reducing redundant multi-copy DNA replication burden (Lean `T27`).
- **Endospore**: Metabolically dormant, stress-resistant bacterial survival capsule (e.g., *Bacillus*, *Clostridium*) formed under starvation or environmental hostility.
- **Escape-Breeding Paradox**: Empirical complex-systems phenomenon wherein intermediate (50%) adversarial restraints act as directional selective pressure breeding covert evasion strategies (kill-switch deletions, dormancy spores, HGT smuggling, metabolic bypasses), sometimes yielding higher vitality than unrestrained baselines.
- **Eukaryote**: Complex compartmentalized cell possessing a double-membrane nucleus, endomembrane system (ER/Golgi/lysosomes), dynamic cytoskeleton, and endosymbiotic bioenergetic organelles (mitochondria/plastids).
- **Eutely**: Developmental strategy (exhibited by tardigrades ~1,000 cells, *C. elegans* 959 somatic cells, rotifers ~1,000 cells, and dicyemids 20–40 cells) in which adult somatic cell count is fixed and post-embryonic growth occurs purely by cellular hypertrophy rather than mitosis (Lean `T41`).
- **Exosome / Asymmetric Budding**: Shedding of a smaller membrane-bound daughter vesicle from a parent cell in the swollen reduced-volume regime $1/2 < Q < 1$ where symmetric binary fission is geometrically impossible (Lean `T15`–`T16`).

### F
- **First Law of Thermodynamics (Exact Integer Closure)**: Total conserved energy across chemical bonds, transmembrane PMF, local thermal pool, and radiated heat sink equals initial energy plus explicit external inflow minus export with `0 uq` integer residual (Lean `T02`, `T08`).
- **Flame Cell (Protonephridium)**: Specialized ciliated excretory cell in microscopic metazoans (rotifers, flatworms, gastrotrichs) that actively pumps excess water and metabolic waste out of the body cavity to prevent osmotic rupture (Lean `T43`).

### G
- **Gap Junction (Connexin / Innexin)**: Direct intercellular transmembrane ion channel coupling cytoplasmic voltages ($V_m$) and small signaling molecules between adjacent cells in a multicellular tissue; passive relaxation conserves charge and dissipates Dirichlet energy (Lean `T31`–`T33`).
- **GDGT (Glycerol Dialkyl Glycerol Tetraether)**: Membrane-spanning bipolar covalent archaeal lipid forming a rigid monolayer resistant to hyperthermophilic and extreme acid lysis.
- **Elementary Sextic Budding Inequality (`T16`)**: Machine-checked algebraic identity in `MembraneGeometry.lean` proving $2(x^3 + y^3)^2 - (x^2 + y^2)^3 = (x - y)^2(x^4 + 2x^3y + 2xy^3 + y^4) \ge 0$ for integers $x, y > 0$, with strict inequality for $x \neq y$ and equality iff $x = y$ on the cube/square vesicle proxy model. No mathematical originality is claimed for this elementary factorization.
- **Great Oxidation Event (GOE)**: Planetary-scale geochemical transition driven by oxygenic phototrophs regenerating ambient terminal electron acceptor (`X` / $\text{O}_2$) from low-energy waste (`W`), unlocking high-yield aerobic respiration and eukaryogenesis.

### H
- **Hachimoji DNA/RNA**: Synthetic 8-letter genetic polymer (4 natural + 4 synthetic nucleobases) demonstrating that Watson–Crick hereditary storage is not limited to Earth's 4-letter alphabet (Hoshika et al., 2019).
- **Heteroplasmy**: Coexistence of genetically distinct organelle populations (e.g., cooperative vs. selfish mutant mitochondria) inside a single eukaryotic host; uneven mitotic partitioning amplifies inter-daughter variance so host-level selection can purge cheaters (Lean `T25`–`T26`).
- **Horizontal Gene Transfer (HGT)**: Non-vertical acquisition of extracellular DNA/plasmids (transformation), viral DNA (transduction), or direct cell-to-cell transfer (conjugation).
- **Hydrostatic Skeleton**: Biomechanical support structure in soft-bodied microscopic metazoans where incompressible fluid inside a sealed hemocoel/pseudocoel converts circumferential muscle contraction into axial body elongation (Lean `T40`).

### J–L
- **JCVI-syn3A**: Genomically minimal synthetic bacterium (~493 genes, ~543 kbp) engineered by the J. Craig Venter Institute and modeled in 4D whole-cell simulations by the Luthey-Schulten lab (Hutchison et al., 2016; Breuer et al., 2019; Pelletier et al., 2021; Thornburg et al., 2022).
- **Lane Prokaryotic Bioenergetic Ceiling**: Nick Lane & William Martin's (2010) scaling law demonstrating that outer-membrane ATP production scales as surface area ($R^2$) while cytoplasmic maintenance scales as volume ($R^3$), forcing $A/V \propto 1/R$ starvation above a critical prokaryotic radius until internalized endosymbionts restore $O(1)$ power density (Lean `T20`–`T22`).
- **Markov Blanket**: Statistical and physical boundary (the semi-permeable plasma membrane) separating internal cellular states from external environmental states, interacting only through active transport/sensory channels (Lean `T36`).
- **Mitochondrion**: Double-membrane respiratory organelle executing oxidative phosphorylation across folded inner cristae; descended from an $\alpha$-proteobacterial endosymbiont.
- **Mitophagy**: Selective autophagic destruction and biomass recycling of damaged or selfish non-exporting mitochondria by the eukaryotic host.
- **MscL / MscS (Mechanosensitive Channels)**: Membrane-tension-gated safety-valve channels that open under high osmotic turgor to release solutes and prevent catastrophic osmotic burst.
- **Myoepithelial Cell**: Dual-function cell combining outer epithelial barrier protection with basal contractile filaments, enabling sub-500-cell metazoan compression (Lean `T39`).
