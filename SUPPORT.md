# Support (`SUPPORT.md`)

## Where to Get Help

1. **Documentation & FAQ first:** Check [`README.md`](README.md), [`docs/faq.md`](docs/faq.md), [`docs/glossary.md`](docs/glossary.md), [`REPLICATION.md`](REPLICATION.md), and [`docs/configuration.md`](docs/configuration.md).
2. **Questions, ideas & replication reports:** Open a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/0thernes/cellulogonic-mecha/discussions) or an issue using the structured templates in `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/`.
3. **Bugs, formal proof challenges & falsification reports:** Use the **Bug or finding** issue form (selecting one of the 10 finding classes defined in `docs/glossary.md`) and include your OS, compiler/Lean version, exact command, and SHA-256 digest.
4. **Private security reports:** Follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) — never disclose vulnerabilities in public issues.
